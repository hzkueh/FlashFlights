# 10 — Motion and visual polish

**What to build:** Live changes read as smooth and legible rather than as things blinking in and out. The app looks like a considered product in both themes.

**Blocked by:** 07, 08.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Framer Motion transitions on the seat map as Seats change status — animated, not instant swaps.
- [ ] Countdown treatments for the Hold timer and sale windows, with visible urgency as time runs low.
- [ ] Both themes look deliberate; no unstyled or contrast-broken states.
- [ ] Loading, empty, and error states exist on every page.
- [ ] Responsive down to mobile widths; the seat map stays usable on a small screen.
- [ ] Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
